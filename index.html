Smart Canteen Food Ordering Platform for SVCE Students

QBite transforms campus dining at Sri Venkateshwara College of Engineering (SVCE) by eliminating long counter queues and wait times. Students can browse the complete college canteen menu from anywhere on campus, place orders online, and pick up hot, freshly prepared meals with digital token verification.

Online Food Ordering for SVCE Students

Order fresh South Indian breakfast (idli, vada, masala dosa, poori), afternoon hot meals, North Indian combos, evening snacks, and refreshing hot/cold beverages directly from classrooms or campus hostels.

Schedule Your Order in Advance

Pre-order meals ahead of class intervals or college lunch breaks. Select a future pickup date and time slot within canteen operating hours so your food is fresh and waiting without queueing.

Live Kitchen Tokens & Real-Time Queue

Watch your live order token update in real-time from 'Pending' to 'Preparing' to 'Ready for Pickup'. Tokens are also broadcast to the digital TV board at the canteen collection counter.

Kitchen Management & Instant Counter Pickup

A specialized staff dashboard enables canteen chefs to prepare meals efficiently, sequence incoming orders, and hand over freshly packed food instantly at the counter.
